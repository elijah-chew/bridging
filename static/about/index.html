About Bridging

Elijah Chew · Founder · Free O-Level E-Math for Singapore students

Bridging is a free O-Level E-Math practice site for Singapore secondary students—topic practice, notes, and a daily question aligned to the SEAB syllabus. Student founder Elijah Chew built it while preparing for O-Levels and tutoring, so good revision does not sit behind a paywall.

Why he built Bridging

The idea for Bridging came while he was sitting O-Levels and had started tutoring. Good E-Math practice was locked behind paid tools. Bridging is the product he shipped so a student can revise without paying for the hint.

Bridging is topic practice, notes, and a daily E-Math question, aligned to the SEAB syllabus. It is not affiliated with SEAB or the Ministry of Education, and it is designed for Singaporean students.

What he has shipped

He spent more than 200 hours building Bridging and writing notes across 17 topics. The site passed 100 signups and kept growing past 110 students using it to practise E-Math.

He also built BentoTile, a student portfolio product, with Ryusei N. and Benjamin Koh. BentoTile was named one of 10 promising youth startups at the Singapore AI Youth Festival 2026. Bridging is the product he keeps building for students who need E-Math practice they can actually open.
