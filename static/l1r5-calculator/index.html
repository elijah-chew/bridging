How the aggregates work

L1R5 (JC today): L1 is English or Higher Mother Tongue. The five relevant subjects must include a humanities subject and a mathematics or science subject. Religious Knowledge cannot be used. You cannot count both Higher Mother Tongue and Mother Tongue.

L1R4 from 2028: L1 is G3 English or Higher Mother Tongue. R1 is humanities, R2 is mathematics or science, R3 is another humanities/math/science subject, R4 is any other eligible subject. JC needs net L1R4 ≤ 16; MI needs ≤ 20. Bonus points from CCA, Higher Mother Tongue, and affiliation are capped at 3 (plus 2 for LEP if you take that programme).

ELR2B2 (polytechnic): English plus two relevant subjects (type A, B, C, or D) plus two other best subjects, minus up to 2 CCA points. From 2028, EL/R1/R2/B1 stay on the G3 scale and B2 uses a G2 equivalent (G3 A1–B3 maps to 1, B4–C6 to 2, D7 to 3, E8 to 4, 9 excluded). Limit 22, or 24 for Nursing.

ITE today uses ELB4-A, ELR1B3-B, or ELR2B2-C on G3 grades. ELMAB3 (often called EMB3) is English + Math + 3 best subjects on the G2 scale — used for PFP and for 2-year Higher Nitec from 2028 (limit 19). G1 subjects do not count in these JC/poly/Higher Nitec aggregates.
