Reference

How the aggregates work

Short version of the MOE posting rules used above.

  • L1R5 (JC today) — English plus five relevant G3 subjects (include humanities and math/science). Higher Mother Tongue is pass/fail only; tick the bonus above.
  • L1R4 (from 2028) — G3 English, humanities, math/science, and two other eligible subjects. Net ≤16 for JC, ≤20 for MI; CCA, HMTL, and affiliation bonus capped at 3 (+2 for LEP).
  • ELR2B2 (poly) — English, two relevant subjects (type A–D), and two best subjects, minus up to 2 CCA. From 2028: limit 22 (24 Nursing); B2 may use a G2-mapped grade.
  • ITE — ELB4, ELR1B3, or ELR2B2 on G3 today; ELMAB3 on the G2 scale for PFP and 2028 Higher Nitec (≤19). G1 grades are not used in these aggregates.

This calculator follows MOE posting rules as published for JAE and the 2028 admissions changes. It does not check course-specific minimum entry grades. Confirm figures against the latest MOE JAE booklet.
